How a reading becomes proof

  1. SenseTemperature, humidity, gas and jolts, every few minutes, in a sealed box that rides with the produce.
  2. SignA security chip signs each reading. Its key can't be copied out, not even by us.
  3. ChainEach record carries the fingerprint of the one before it, so nothing can be edited, deleted or reordered unseen.
  4. Survive dead zonesNo signal on the highway? Records keep chaining on the board and sync, encrypted, when it's back.
  5. AnchorThe newest signed record goes on Polygon. The smart contract checks the chip's signature itself before accepting it.
  6. VerifyThe buyer scans the QR on the lid. Their phone re-checks every reading. No app, no login, no trust in us.
